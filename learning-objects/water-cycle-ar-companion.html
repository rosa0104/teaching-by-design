Water Cycle AR Companion
Grades 3–6 Science • Reusable Learning Object

Predict • Explore • Explain the Water Cycle

This interactive object prepares students for an augmented-reality water-cycle station and helps them explain what they observe afterward. No headset is required.

Learning Objective: After completing the object, students will identify and sequence evaporation, condensation, precipitation, and collection and explain how heat from the Sun drives the cycle.

Keyboard friendlyImmediate feedbackNo color-only directionsReusableScreen-based alternative

5. Check your understanding

1. Which process changes liquid water into water vapor?

2. Why do clouds form?

3. Which statement best explains the role of the Sun?

6. Explain what you learned

Complete this sentence aloud or in writing:

“The Sun helps drive the water cycle because ____________________. After water evaporates, it ____________________.”

Students may respond orally, type, draw a labeled diagram, or use speech-to-text.

Teacher note: Designed as a companion to a small-group AR science station such as the Merge EDU water-cycle experience proposed in the Flexible Digital Learning & Innovation Lab.

Accessibility features: semantic headings, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, adjustable text, high contrast, text feedback, and a non-AR alternative.
